Symbols:

  0xffffffd603f15218 = heap-range dynamic lock (an event's inner or an

Taskdump at the wedge (dfx=taskdump):
  pid 1 (fsfuzz): RUNNING, preempt=259 (0x103 = SOFTIRQ|PREEMPT)
  idle @0xffffffff80caebd0 (ti_cpu=1): preempt=257 (0x101)
  -> BOTH CPUs were inside timer-softirq processing (softirq_tick runs
     on whatever task the tick interrupted; here pid1 and the idle task).

Code defect (kernel/src/perf/mod.rs):
  1. softirq_tick(): holds REGISTRY.lock_irqsave() across the whole
     event iteration; INSIDE that section it (a) takes ev.inner.lock()
     and (b) calls to_wake.push() -- A VEC ALLOCATION UNDER REGISTRY.
  2. Inside ev.inner.lock(), push_sample() grows the ring VecDeque --
     ANOTHER ALLOCATION UNDER A SPINLOCK (inner).
  3. register(): reg.push() -- allocation under REGISTRY (open path).
  Under the fuzzer's constant heap pressure (the standing ENOMEM/EMFILE
  storm class; the 128MB kernel heap crawls), each under-lock
  allocation enters the allocator slowpath while the OTHER CPU spins
  on REGISTRY (softirq) or inner -- a watchdog-tripping multi-CPU spin
  stall. The 67/67 alternation is the two CPUs trading these roles.

Fix direction (perf owner):
  - to_wake: fixed-size array (events are few) or collect ids without
    allocation; NEVER allocate under REGISTRY.
  - ring: reserve capacity at open time and CAP growth there (RING_CAP
    bound already exists as a byte budget; make it a preallocated
    capacity), so push_sample never allocates under inner.
  - register(): pre-reserve the registry Vec once at init (ids are
    bounded by fd count) or use a fixed table.
  Rule of thumb: no allocation inside any spinlock section reached
  from softirq/irq context (lock-ordering doc territory).
