BROKEN (the remaining root cause — kernel, likely sched/timer/ipc):
- The frame pipeline starves system-wide: composer vsync worker
  (libdisplay_composer_vendor.z.so DrmVsyncWorker = usleep(16000) +
  clock_gettime + pthread condvar, NO drm vblank ioctls) bursts at

  A 16ms sleep occasionally takes ~0.5-0.7s IN-PROCESS under the
  loaded OH system — kernel timer/scheduling outlier under load.
  Consequence: ~6-8 bootanimation frames per ~10 min window (want
  150 at 30fps), RS repaints ~11 — screen stays effectively black.
- render_service dies late (~2x per boot): SIGABRT in cc12, SIGSEGV
  in cc13 (no user-mode trap dump). usb_host also SIGSEGV'd in cc13.
  Suspect the same timer/wake family or the known scribbler class.
- RS->client vsync uses NO socketpair (shim saw none) — binder or
  shared-memory based; the bootanimation's 30Hz subscription gets
  ticks at ~1/min.
