rt-tests latency & scheduling stress

The cyclictest round record, the hackbench matrix, pi_stress status and the injection protocol · Canonical: the qa-suites run/qa-suites-findings.md

§1cyclictest (rt-tests v2.11, static musl)

Protocol: cyclictest -D 30 -i 1000 -m (SCHED_OTHER) and -m -p 98 (SCHED_FIFO); a 30 s window, a 1000 µs interval, mlockall, 30k cycles. Rig as on the fio page (q35/KVM -cpu max smp2/2G, virtio-blk, one boot per round).

§3pi_stress

  • r1-r4: FAIL — the FUTEX_LOCK_PI family was unimplemented then (ENOSYS).
  • The 2026-11-17 re-run (current main, PI futexes merged in 0b4d1752b): the glibc PI-mutex initialization path returns ENOTSUP ("initializing mutex: Operation not supported") — the kernel-side FUTEX_LOCK_PI exists, but glibc's PTHREAD_PRIO_INHERIT mutex dependency chain (robust registration + PI semantics) is not fully traversed yet; a registered UAPI compatibility gap, with pi_stress passing as the acceptance target.

§4Toolchain and injection

  • The rt-tests v2.11 upstream tarball is committed; musl-static builds (run/rttests-bin/cyclictest, hackbench; pi_stress is a host-glibc build); the build carries a numa shim and the musl sched_* ENOSYS-stub override (F-3: the toolchain's libc.a ships ENOSYS stubs for the sched family — which once stopped cyclictest at "unable to get scheduler parameters", resolved by the override).
  • Every QA boot injects the three binaries automatically; a standalone run: test/qrtests/run-suite-x86.sh "60 cyclictest -D 30 -i 1000 -m".
  • schedprobe (injected on every QA boot) verifies the raw sched_setparam/getscheduler/setscheduler syscall surface — the F-3 evidence probe.
