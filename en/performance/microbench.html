§4The context-switch cost anatomy (cctx segments, a mechanism note)

  • The x86_64 switch machinery ~620 ns (schedule 125 + the GRQ lock 102 + pick 96 + the mm switch 202–295 + FPU 34 + FS/GS 54 + GPRs ~22) — the reference kernel's native switch machinery ~910 ns; after the fdget borrow + getppid O(1), the pbench p2_ctxsw pipe ping-pong reads 0.090–0.105 µs.
  • The wake-side segments (WK_SEL/WK_ENQ/WK_DISP/IPI_N/IPI_NS) and the GRQ-hold decomposition are on scheduler §8; the NOHZ hostile-load A/B (0/10→11/12) on interrupts & timers §6.
Canonical: docs/development/perf-gate-baseline.md (anchors and bands), docs/development/perf-report.md (the pbench design). The Linux columns of pbench and the flush wall come from the two-sided battery (run/bench-pair-1117/, pbench-pair); each row carries its newest measured value.
