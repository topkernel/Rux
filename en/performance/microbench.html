§8Tier 2: pbench P1–P7 anchors (SMP-8 standard, re-banked 2026-11-08)

The SMP-8 standard (user directive 2026-11-07): every launch boots with at least 8 CPUs (Kernel.toml smp.max_cpus = 8; the three-arch drivers default to -smp 8). The old anchors were measured at 2–4 real CPUs and cannot judge an 8-CPU run — cross-configuration comparison is as meaningless as debug-vs-strong. The anchors below = the SMP-8 same-rig A/B baseline (base 166e4516 vs per-mm-break c127e4bc, KVM q35 4G).

Open cost (stated plainly): the COW fault's +58% at SMP-8 (12.07→18.42 µs) is the price of the SMP-8 standard, recorded as an honest gap in the safety-performance ledger; it matters for fork-heavy service graphs (OH/GNOME). The next optimization target is the COW fault path's cross-CPU shootdown, not the break handling itself. The four-configuration comparison (8/4 CPUs × base/fix) is in the baseline file's "Re-bank: SMP-8 standard" section.

§4The NOHZ A/B (scheduling strands under a hostile load)

  • 4+4 interleaved lmbench lat_syscall,null boots (a 35 s budget, 6 hostile external QEMUs): the main tip passes 0/10; the churn fix alone 5/12 (42%); nohz_full=1: 11/12 (92%); the p1_null median 0.050 vs 0.060 µs (−17%); both census bands hold exactly (TERM 8 / REFUSED 32).
  • Implementation: a function-for-function mirror of the reference tick-sched.c (details on interrupts & timers §6).

§5The context-switch cost anatomy (cctx segments)

  • Per segment: the x86_64 switch machinery ~620 ns (schedule 125 + the GRQ lock 102 + pick 96 + the mm switch 202–295 + FPU 34 + FS/GS 54 + GPRs ~22) — the reference kernel's native switch machinery ~910 ns.
  • After the fdget borrow + getppid O(1): the pbench p2_ctxsw pipe ping-pong 0.090–0.105 µs (from the 1.24–2.15 class, −90%+) — the sharpest scheduling-latency reading on file.
  • The wake-side segments (WK_SEL/WK_ENQ/WK_DISP/IPI_N/IPI_NS) and the GRQ-hold decomposition (runqueue accounting / dequeue-enqueue / pick / stats) are on scheduler §8.
