LTP TIMEOUT-class audit

2026-11-07 · P0-class rule: a TIMEOUT is a "cannot-run" verdict, prioritized over optimization work · source doc docs/test/ltp-timeout-audit.md
Scope: every 13 TIMEOUT + 1 WEDGE row of the r27 census (1,869-case list, main@e23d3c3f, x86_64+KVM). Method: each row re-run one-test-per-boot on current main (scripts/test/ltpscan/scan-x86_64.py --chunk 1; the in-guest runner gives each test a 30 s budget, then pid-1 SIGTERMs it and records rc=143), with the LTP 20240524 sources open beside the serials.

§3Follow-ups

  1. Census hygiene: the 9 by-design resident helpers should carry a BY-DESIGN marker in future censuses (runner-side list or verdict post-processing) so they stop reading as kernel TIMEOUT regressions.
  2. The r28 census: will pick up the ptrace06 PASS and these reclassifications.
  3. ht_affinity: a per-step timing dig (affinity syscall + signal handshake cost).
  4. openfile: rides the lost-wake producer hunt; the rescue machinery bounds its damage to sub-second.
