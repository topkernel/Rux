r1–r10 (riscv64·TCG, previous host) → r14–r20 (same rig, closing at 1028) → r21 onward the x86_64+KVM standing rig (first round 1010 → r27 1084). Mid-series dips were mostly host-load noise, recovered through isolated re-runs.

§2Methodology

  • Flake isolation: new full-scan failures are isolated-rerun twice before being called regressions — host load produces timeout/precision flakes (known: accept02, getrusage04, kill02, genfmod, rename/statfs families).
  • NOT-OURS ledger: the same musl LTP binaries run on the host Linux kernel; identical failures are recorded as test/environment issues rather than kernel gaps (decoded case by case in verdicts-r27.txt).
  • WEDGE rescue: 51 WEDGE/POISON rows from the raw r27 scan were rescued via isolation boots and spliced back; 6 stubborn rows re-ran one-case-per-boot; mmap1 stays WEDGE per the standing adjudication.
  • Every round's full results, deltas and verdicts are archived in the ltp-r27 lane's results/ directory; TIMEOUT-class cases get top-priority dedicated lanes.
