Changelog (highlights)

The full history: docs/progress/changelog.md and git log — this page records structural milestones only

2026-11-06

  • The munmap churn-race family closed (taxonomy row 34): the detach-all-then-zap rework landed, closing the chunked tail-TLB-flush races; the x86_64 fix (c4a548d7) ported back to riscv64/aarch64; the OH crash storm of ~66 SEGVs/boot verified 3/3 clean by the mmap-witness probe.
  • The GNOME desktop regression retrospective published: both root causes fixed (the munmap races; ext4 async read-fill cross-file page-cache poisoning gated off pending its own lane); a desktop-boot gate and byte-exact mmap verification probes move into the standing surface.
  • The safety ledger round 4: taxonomy row 34 + boundary-row extensions.

2026-11-05

  • LTP r27 = 1084/1869 (x86_64+KVM): +17, the pidfd family all green; 51 WEDGE rows rescued via isolation boots.
  • PI futexes land fully in main: LOCK/TRYLOCK/UNLOCK_PI + the requeue pair (21 probes × 2), the futex lock order extended with INV-LOCK-PI-1.

2026-11-03

  • The safety-and-performance ledger (docs/architecture/safety-and-performance.md) and the Rust-native register (rust-native-register.md) established: every merge round files its safety properties, performance numbers and eliminated bug classes in the same change.
  • Bug taxonomy v1: all 3,358 deduplicated commits adjudicated one by one; 34 memory-corruption defects attributed (23 audited unsafe/asm, 11 safe but non-pointer-expression classes).
  • The typed timer wheel (D-19) A/B: nanosleep 772.6→662.7 ms, p2_ctxsw 12.198 vs 15.400 µs.

2026-11-01

  • The GDM greeter achieved (x86_64+KVM, ~4.5 s boot-to-login, 45 minutes zero panic); x86_64+KVM becomes the standing test rig; the fs metadata family re-anchors (−65%~−88%); kernel suite 1267/1267 (riscv64), 1263/1263 (x86_64).
  • IF=0 re-anchored at 20.4%±3pp (the mechanism: softirqs leave the IF=0 window to ksoftirqd).

2026-10-30/31

  • aarch64 lands in main: three architectures complete — interactive shell + signals + the suite 1119/1119 green.
  • OpenHarmony /data mounted + SystemUI lock screen: after the ext4 superblock 32-bit checksum fix; StartUser(100) in 64 ms.
  • Attribution corrected: the OH watchdog self-kill was a pure performance wall — the kernel's binder was not at fault.

2026-10-07 ~ 10-28

  • x86_64 lands in main (10-07); GNOME Shell renders (10-14) → a complete frame across three boots (10-27) → a typed conversation verified pixel-exact (10-28).
  • The OpenHarmony boot animation renders (10-24); the riscv64 LTP line climbs 801 → 945 → 1028 (closing 10-31).
  • Scheduling strands ended: spin-with-preemption-off convicted as the root cause, strands 233→31; census/heal ships.
  • RVV support mainlined: lazy V-extension enable + per-task vstate.

2026-09-29

  • Ubuntu 22.04 (riscv64) full userspace first boot: a desktop session, bash, D-Bus, ptys, SysV IPC, SMP4.
